This revision: four features, nothing else

The previous plan showed nine features in fourteen scenes. Every one got twenty seconds, which is long enough to see a screen and too short to understand it. This one shows four, and gives each of them two to three times the screen time:

🎓 Timetable · 📄 Owing · ⭐ What to do first · 👥 Friends

Cut entirely: the swap, capture and the five dials, the five-area check-in, recovery, Tonight, the collision warning, the rebalancer and the decline drafter. They stay in the README, which is where Ideation is marked anyway.

One thing carried over on purpose. load = hours × dread is not a feature and gets no demo — but it is the reason Owing and the priority list are not an ordinary to-do app, so it stays as one sentence in the presenter's opening. Cut that sentence and the four features below read like a calendar.

The rubric — and exactly what full marks says

Presentation is 15% of the total. Green is the band this plan is built to land in.

Criterion%0–12–3Full marks
Clarity of explanation5%Confusing; idea not understandable.Understandable but patchy.4–5 · The idea is explained clearly and is easy to grasp.
Structure and flow4%Disorganised, hard to follow.Some structure but jumps around.4 · Logical, well-paced flow from problem to solution.
Delivery and confidence4%Hesitant or flat, hard to engage with.Reasonably confident, some energy.4 · Confident, engaging, and convincing.
Engagement and persuasiveness2%0 · Doesn't make the idea feel worth building.1 · Somewhat compelling.2 · Makes the idea feel genuinely valuable and exciting.
Why four features scores better than nine. Clarity is the biggest single line at 5%, and it is awarded for the idea being easy to grasp — not for coverage. Nine features in five minutes is a list; four features shown end to end is an argument. Structure and flow (4%) is the same bet: four blocks in one order is a shape a viewer can hold.

The host's hint, quoted

"Your Ideation (25%) is judged solely from your README.md, not your video, which means you do not have to spend time in your video discussing all the ideas you had and why they were dropped. Spend the 5 minutes you have to explain your idea to the viewer, why it is novel, how it will be built, and why it deserves to be built. You will lose marks for Presentation (15%) if your video exceeds the 5 minute limit (as stated in the rubrics)."

Cast, locations, kit

Cast — two people is now enough

  • PRESENTER. Your most confident speaker. On camera in scenes 2, 7, 8; in voice-over throughout. Same outfit all day.
  • STUDENT A. The drama lead, scenes 1 and 6a. No dialogue at all — cast whoever is most relaxed on camera, not the best talker.
  • The two extras from the old plan are no longer needed.

Locations — three

  • DESK / BEDROOM, night. Scenes 1, 6a, 8 end.
  • OUTDOOR or plain wall, day. Scenes 2, 7, 8 — every presenter piece.
  • LAPTOP / desk, day. Scene 7 inserts.

The library and corridor shoots are gone with the scenes that used them.

Kit. A phone on a tripod is fine in 1080p. What matters: the microphone — record narration separately, indoors, into earbuds or a cheap lav, never an on-camera mic outdoors. And daylight on the face: stand facing a window, or with the sun behind the camera.

Before you capture a single screen — read this

  1. Open the app → You → Reset. (It used to be under Areas. That screen is gone.) Confirm Home reads 47% and "Doing fine". Every number spoken in this script comes from that state.
  2. The evening question only appears after 8pm. Scene 5d needs it on screen, so capture that beat after 20:00 — or set the device clock forward. It is the only time-gated thing in the app.
  3. Scene 6c and 6g open WhatsApp for real. Let the share sheet appear, then cut. Nothing sends unless you press send.
  4. Scene 6f: /plan-together opens with Amin and Ravi picked and Dinner · 2h. Leave them — that is the state that produces three windows.

Rehearse against the real footage — docs/video/clips/player.html

Every demo beat below has been recorded from the live app at exactly the timings in this plan. Open that file in a browser, press space, and read the script aloud beside it. If you finish a scene before its bar does you are too fast; if the clock turns red you are over five minutes.

  • Camera scenes hold a card for their real length, so the whole 4:37 can be rehearsed, not only the demos.
  • Click any scene in the right-hand column to rehearse it alone. #scene=6 or #t=200 in the address bar does the same.
  • Regenerate after any change with npm run clips, or node scripts/clips.mjs --player-only to rebuild just the page.

The recording drives the real app over Chrome DevTools — it taps the flags, presses "Book 2 sittings for me", toggles the sharing screen. Two things it fakes, both deliberately: the clock is pinned to 21:00 so Home shows the evening question, and window.open is stubbed so the WhatsApp beats do not navigate away mid-capture.

Edit and performance

Assembly order

  1. Lay the eight narration files end to end in order. That is your spine, and its total length is your runtime — check it is under 4:40 before you cut a single picture.
  2. Drop the three presenter pieces onto their lines, synced by waveform.
  3. Fill with demo capture, beat by beat. Speed screen recordings to 1.2× — UI always plays slower than it feels.
  4. Cut the two drama beats in last.
  5. One instrumental bed, low. Lift it at each feature title; drop it out completely under the final line.

Performance — this is the 4% for Delivery

  • Smile on the first line of scene 2. It is audible, and it buys you the opening.
  • Eyes on the lens, never on your own preview. The biggest tell of a nervous pitch.
  • Four features means four gear changes. Land a beat of silence before "And then the one that isn't about work at all" — it is the turn the whole video is built around.
  • Slow on numbers: "seventy-eight", "eleven days", "eight hundred and eighty-one".
  • Scene 8 is slower and quieter than everything before it. Conviction, not volume.
  • Fluffed a line? Re-say it cleanly and keep rolling — fixing it in the edit costs eight seconds.

If the cut lands over 4:50 — trim in this order

  1. Scene 4e (−5s) — drop the "+1h" tap. The sittings booking already made the point.
  2. Scene 6g (−4s) — drop /find-friends and the last line of scene 6.
  3. Scene 1 (−4s) — open on the blank document instead of the clock.
  4. Scene 3a (−4s) — shorten the paste; land on the finished preview list.
  5. Scene 5c (−5s) — cut "Could move" and go straight from the ranked list to the evening question.

Never cut: the 78% attendance card (0:58) · "book my sittings for me" (1:42) · the evening question (2:31) · the whole of scene 6 up to and including the sharing screen (2:44–3:40) · the three-week plan graphic (3:57). That graphic is the only place the video answers "how will it be built", which is one of the host's four jobs.

Five ways teams lose Presentation marks

  1. Running over. The host says it plainly: over five minutes loses marks. Export, watch, check the runtime.
  2. Explaining the ideation. Marked from the README. Here it is a minute you cannot afford.
  3. Narrating the UI. "Now I click here…" — say what it means; let the screen show the click.
  4. Showing everything. The reason this plan has four features and not nine. Coverage is not a criterion; clarity is.
  5. Trailing off. End on the line and the held card. No "…so, yeah, thanks for watching."
