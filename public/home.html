Ballast

A planner for students that measures your week by how much it takes out of you, not how many hours it fills.

  • 🔋 One battery for your week. Classes, deadlines, shifts and plans, each priced by how draining it is.
  • ⚖️ Lighten a heavy week. See what could move, and write the message that moves it.
  • 👥 Plan with friends. Share your free time, not your calendar, and find an evening that suits everyone.
  • 📅 Bring in Google Calendar. Read-only. Your events are filled in; you say how draining each one is.
