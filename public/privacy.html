Privacy policy

Ballast · Last updated 10 October 2026

Ballast is a weekly planner for students. This page says what it keeps, why, who can see it, and how to remove it. Questions: kcchung82@gmail.com.

What Ballast keeps

  • Your account: name, email address and a securely hashed password (or a guest account with no email).
  • Your week: the tasks, classes, check-ins and plans you add, so they are there on any device you sign in on.
  • Your circle: who you are friends with, groups you are in, and plans you send or answer.

What your friends can see

Only what you choose to share: your battery level, a short note you write yourself, and the free time you decide to publish. They never see your tasks, your timetable or your calendar events, and you can hide your free time from anyone.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Ballast asks Google for read-only access to your calendars (calendar.readonly) and your email address, so it can show which account is connected.

  • Ballast reads your upcoming events (about four weeks ahead) when the app asks for them. Events are not stored on Ballast's server.
  • Events you choose to set up become items in your own week. Nobody else sees them.
  • Ballast never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google Calendar.
  • The access token Google issues is stored encrypted, and is used only to read your calendar for you.
  • Disconnecting in the app tells Google to revoke access and deletes the token. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Ballast's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

AI suggestions

Some suggestions (message drafts, recovery ideas, reasons on the rebalance and priority screens) are written by Google's Gemini model. Only the few details each suggestion needs are sent, such as a task's title, its day and how draining you rated it. Your Google Calendar data is never sent to an AI model.

What Ballast does not do

  • No advertising, no selling or renting of data, no tracking across other apps or sites.

Where it is kept

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon and served through Vercel, both over encrypted connections.

Deleting your data

Email kcchung82@gmail.com from the address on your account and everything linked to it will be deleted within 30 days. Disconnecting Google Calendar in the app removes the calendar connection immediately.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
